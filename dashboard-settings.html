Backup & Restore

Owner only. Protect your home's data.

Only the home owner can make or restore backups.

Back up now

Saves a copy of the whole home in your Supabase account. Elara also makes one automatically every day (the last 14 are kept).

Download a backup file

Keep a copy on your computer or phone.

Restore from a file

Replaces the home's data with a backup file. Everyone in the home sees the restored data.

Export Data
