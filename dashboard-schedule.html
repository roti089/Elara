Sync from your calendar

Elara adds the events from your phone or computer calendar and never removes any existing ones. Events already imported are skipped.

From a calendar file (.ics)

Phone: open the calendar app, share or export the calendar as a file, then choose it here. Computer: Google Calendar > Settings > Import & export > Export; Outlook > Save calendar.

From your calendar's private link

Google: Calendar settings > your calendar > Secret address in iCal format. iPhone/iCloud: share the calendar as a public link. Outlook: Settings > Shared calendars > Publish > ICS link.
