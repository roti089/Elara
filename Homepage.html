Elara Home Management System

Elara is a smart home management system designed to simplify and improve the way households manage their daily operations. It focuses on efficient inventory control, allowing users to track, update, and monitor items in real time. With features such as automated alerts, reporting, and voice-enabled commands, Elara provides a modern and intelligent approach to home management. The system aims to enhance convenience, reduce manual effort, and give users better control and visibility over their household resources.
