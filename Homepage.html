WHAT ELARA DOES

Built for the whole household

Tasks that get done

Create chores, assign them only to people in your home, set due times and see who is behind.

Shopping & pantry

One shared list. Bought items move to “In the House”, and receipts and warranties are kept safe.

Who's free, every day

Everyone marks busy, away or available for each day. Adding an event when someone is busy tells them.

Smart home control

Switch lights and plugs (CBi / Smart Life), run Morning, Night and Away routines, and track energy.

Reports per person

See who used how much electricity, spent what on groceries and finished which tasks.

Elara AI & voice

Talk or type: “add milk”, “turn off the lights”, “what can I cook tonight?”

Strict sharing

The owner invites people and decides exactly what each person may see or change.

Backups & notifications

Daily cloud backups and push notifications for the things that matter to you.
